# Atlas typography

Use Spectral Regular for cloud names and article headings, with Spectral Italic for Latin genus names. Set descriptions, photograph captions and article paragraphs in PT Serif Regular. Use the font's natural spacing for continuous reading in Ukrainian and English. Bundle the fonts for offline reading.

The [mobile design study](docs/design/atlas-typography-uk.png) uses 18 px text on a 27 px line for descriptions and paragraphs, and 16 px captions on a 24 px line. Let long names wrap. The study uses Source Sans 3 for navigation labels and counters. Keep the text legible over each genus's pastel background as readers increase the system text size.

## Controls and photograph credits

Use Source Sans 3 for buttons, navigation, counters, photographer names, source links and rights information. Keep the observational caption in PT Serif. On a genus card, keep the credit close to the photograph and leave about 40 px between its final line and the genus heading. Measure from the last line when a credit wraps. In the detail gallery, keep it with the active frame's caption and update both when the reader changes photographs. Set credits in Source Sans 3 Regular (400), 16 px, in dark slate (#27474D). Place the gallery credit directly below its caption with about 8 px of space, and leave a larger gap before the article heading. Allow long credits to wrap.

Let the sky fill the width of the genus card. Crop the preview to suit the screen and fade its lower edge into the genus background. In the detail gallery, show the complete photograph at its original proportions within the text margins. A soft wash of color drawn from the photograph sits just outside its edges. Keep captions and controls on the pastel surface.

Show the photographer and the source collection in both languages. The underlined source name opens the bundled record for that exact photograph, including its title, creator, original source URL, license or public-domain declaration, and modifications. Readers can consult this record offline and follow its original-source link when connected.
