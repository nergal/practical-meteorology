# First release

## Field experience

1. Open the atlas on a mobile device, including on a first launch at sea without a connection.
2. Browse all ten cloud genera through one representative photograph per genus. A beginner can start by visual resemblance without knowing a name or making a forecast.
3. Open a plausible match and browse two more photographs of the same genus. Each photograph has a brief caption pointing out a feature the reader can check in the real sky.
4. Optionally read the article. Explore how the cloud forms, how the sky might evolve and how much the observation supports a local weather outlook. Follow a reference to a related genus when useful.

The article itself explains the relevance of additional observations such as wind or cooling. Readers can compare later changes with the text without entering observations or selecting symptoms.

## Release checks

- All ten genera are available, each with one representative photo, two additional photos and one article. The collection totals 30 photographs and 10 articles.
- Starting with a fresh installation and connectivity disabled, a reader can open the atlas, browse every photograph, read its caption, open every article and navigate between related entries. All required content ships with the build.
- Each entry meets the [editorial guide](atlas-editorial-guide.md). Every photograph has a checked identification and documented permission for offline redistribution.
- Recognition and reading work without an account, automated identification or a live forecasting service. AI and a backend are unnecessary for the agreed experience.
- The implementation deliverable is a built APK. Deployment and distribution belong to the project owner.

## Deferred work and decisions

Photo-sequence lessons, self-assessment and the observation journal are deferred. The article text still teaches changes over time. Mountain-specific forecasting and the broader encyclopedia topics fall outside the initial setting.

Technology stack, architecture and target platform coverage will be discussed separately. This product agreement specifies the mobile experience and requested delivery artifact.
