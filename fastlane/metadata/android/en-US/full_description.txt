Look up at the sky, then swipe through photographs of the ten cloud genera. When a cloud looks familiar, open its gallery to compare it with two more examples.

Each article explains what you can see from the ground, how the cloud forms and what its changes can tell you about the weather. You can open the photographer’s credit and license for each image.

The atlas works offline, including on first launch. Photographs and articles are included in the app, in English and Ukrainian. The atlas follows your device’s language settings.
