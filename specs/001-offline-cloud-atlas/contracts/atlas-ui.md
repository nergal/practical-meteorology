# Atlas interface contract

| Starting view | Action | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First launch | Open the app | The initial genus card appears with its representative photograph, name, credit, description, position and pastel background. |
| Genus card | Swipe horizontally or use navigation controls | The selected genus and all its visible values change together. |
| Genus card | Tap the photo or details action, or swipe down on the card | Details open for the current genus at its representative frame. |
| Genus card or details | Activate the source link | The bundled record for the visible frame opens with its origin and reuse terms. |
| Details | Swipe horizontally over the gallery or use its arrows | Photograph, position, caption and attribution change together. |
| Details | Scroll vertically, including from over the gallery | Scroll to the article and related entries. |
| Details | Follow a related genus | Open the target genus article in the current language. |
| Credit or details | Use back | Restore the originating view and selection. |
| Atlas | Switch language | Update visible text and captions. Preserve the genus and, when applicable, the frame. |

At either sequence boundary, the position and displayed values remain in agreement. Long credits wrap, and spacing before the card heading starts below the last credit line. All first-party actions in this contract are available without a network connection.
